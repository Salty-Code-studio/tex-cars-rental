## Recommended pairing (saltycodestudio default — until brand font confirmed)
For a **budget-but-trustworthy car rental** (clear, fast-scanning, mobile-first, fleet + price tables):

- **Headings / wordmark feel:** a confident geometric or grotesque sans — e.g. **Clash Display**,
  **Satoshi**, or **Inter Tight** — bold, wide, legible at a glance. Avoid anything fussy; rental
  shoppers scan for price + car + "book."
- **Body / UI:** **Inter** (or **Albert Sans**) — neutral, excellent number legibility for rate
  tables and phone numbers.
- **Numbers:** ensure tabular figures for the rate table.

> Lock this only after the owner confirms whether a brand font already exists. If the logo carries a
> distinct typeface, echo it in headings and keep Inter for body/UI.
